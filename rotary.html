What Is Rotary?

A worldwide network of people of action

Rotary connects neighbours, friends, professionals, leaders, and problem-solvers who work together to improve communities, promote goodwill, and advance peace.

Rotary clubs contribute experience, mentorship, professional networks, and long-term partnerships. Rotaract clubs contribute energy, innovation, creativity, and youth-led leadership to the same global movement.

Our shared purpose
Service becomes more powerful when experience, fresh ideas, and community relationships work together.

  • Create sustainable solutions through partnership
What Is Rotaract?

Leadership and professional growth through service

Rotaract is a member-led community for adults who want to serve, learn, lead, build meaningful friendships, and make a measurable difference.

Rotary Areas of Focus

Where service creates lasting impact

Rotary and Rotaract projects commonly respond to seven interconnected areas of community need.

Peacebuilding

Promoting dialogue, understanding, conflict prevention, and peaceful communities.

🩺

Disease Prevention

Supporting health education, prevention, treatment, and stronger health systems.

💧

Water and Sanitation

Improving access to safe water, sanitation facilities, and hygiene education.

👩‍🍼

Maternal and Child Health

Supporting healthier mothers, safer births, and improved outcomes for children.

📚

Education and Literacy

Expanding access to learning, literacy, mentorship, and educational resources.

💼

Community Development

Building skills, livelihoods, entrepreneurship, and sustainable local economies.

🌳

Protecting the Environment

Supporting conservation, climate action, clean communities, and environmental awareness.
