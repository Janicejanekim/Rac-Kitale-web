Who We Are

Service brings us together. Fellowship keeps us moving.

Rotaract Club of Kitale is a home for young leaders who want to make a difference while building practical skills, professional connections, and lifelong friendships.

We work with our sponsor Rotary club, community institutions, schools, businesses, and fellow Rotaract clubs to design projects that respond to real local needs.
